# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want VivaMate AI to be a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative project for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences.
- Focus on ideas, code, and solutions rather than individuals.
- Give helpful feedback and accept feedback professionally.
- Protect other people’s privacy and personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would make participation unsafe or uncomfortable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this code. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub’s private contact options.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, issue discussions, pull requests, and other community interactions related to VivaMate AI.
